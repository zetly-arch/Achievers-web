FAQs

Everything about classes, mocks, payments and free trials.

Who are the classes for?
NEB Class 11 and 12 students (Science & Management) plus Medical (CEE) and Engineering (IOE/CSIT) entrance aspirants.
How do live classes work?
Streamed live on a fixed weekly schedule; every class is also recorded for rewatch on web or the Android app.
Are mock tests really free?
Yes — enrolled students get free chapter-wise and full-length mocks with instant results, solutions, percentile and weak-area analysis.
What payment methods are accepted?
eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay and major Nepali bank transfers. Secure, encrypted checkout. No hidden fees · cancel anytime.
Can I try before I pay?
Yes — book a free demo class, take a free mock test, or request free 1-on-1 counselling (Sun–Fri).
Online or physical batches?
Both. Online runs on web + Android app; physical batches at Puspalal Chowk, Mahendranagar have limited seats.
How do I get the Android app?
Get it on Google Play. iOS is coming soon.
How fast is support?
Doubts are cleared live in class and on chat after. Counselling and contact messages get a reply within 24 hours. WhatsApp: 981-0686665.
